# Report Quality Standards

- Reports must be client-readable and technically credible.
- Findings must include evidence, business risk, recommendation, validation steps, confidence, and classification.
- Group repeated findings rather than repeating the same narrative many times.
- Do not overstate compliance or assurance.
- Separate `confirmed-risk` from `requires-validation` and `advisory`.
- Include positive signals and explicit limitations.
- Scores must be explainable and consistent.

## Report Writing Notes
- Executive readers should understand priority, impact, and required decisions quickly.
- Technical readers should be able to trace conclusions back to evidence.
- Coverage gaps and assumptions should be visible, not hidden.
