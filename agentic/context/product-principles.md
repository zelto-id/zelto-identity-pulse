# Product Principles

- Local-first identity posture assessment is the default operating model.
- Auth0 and Okta Workforce are first-class providers.
- Reports must be useful to IAM engineers and business stakeholders.
- No SaaS or backend platform exists yet.
- No write operations are allowed.
- No telemetry is allowed.
- Deterministic findings come before AI-assisted analysis.
- Assessment and remediation-validation workflows come before platform complexity.

## Interpretation
- Prefer portable CLI workflows over hosted orchestration.
- Favor evidence-driven, explainable results over speculative automation.
- Add complexity only when it clearly improves repeatable customer assessments.
