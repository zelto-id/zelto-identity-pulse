Handling & limitations

IMPLEMENTED

Two provider connectors.

Auth0 and Okta collect configuration, normalize snapshots and report collector status. Both clients implement retry and pagination behavior.

IMPLEMENTED WITH LIMITATIONS

Evidence and comparison.

Structured reports, delta comparison and optional framework mappings exist. Missing findings alone do not prove remediation; framework mappings are not a compliance verdict.

PROPOSED EXPERIENCE

The interactive workspace.

Entra ID, Ping Identity and Keycloak illustrate proposed connectors. Their API contracts, rules and scoring are not implemented. The workspace uses fictional examples.
